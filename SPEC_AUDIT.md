# Intelligence-system specification

## Required pipeline

DATA → NORMALIZATION → MULTI-TIMEFRAME STATE → STRUCTURE → LIQUIDITY → SMC EVENTS → MACRO CONTEXT → HISTORICAL ANALOGUES → HYPOTHESES → EVIDENCE → INTELLIGENCE REPORT.

## Nine intelligence workstreams

### 1. Cross-timeframe contradiction engine
Records agreement, conflict and missing evidence across 12M/6M/3M/Weekly/Daily/4H/1H/15M/5M/1M. Weekly is the structural authority.

### 2. Event sequencing
Orders observed events and identifies sequencing problems without treating chronology as proof of causality.

### 3. Inducement model
Requires internal liquidity and subsequent structural/liquidity context. It must not label every liquidity sweep as inducement.

### 4. Historical analogues
Uses feature similarity rather than exact dictionary equality and supports conditional statistics.

### 5. Macro-to-price reaction
Separates actual/expected surprise from subsequent observed price reaction.

### 6. Event studies
Measures fixed-horizon historical responses to defined events. This is descriptive research, not a trading simulator.

### 7. Evidence ledger
Every material claim can be linked to observation, source, timeframe, method, supporting evidence, contradictions and invalidation.

### 8. Intelligence report
Produces competing hypotheses and explicit evidence/invalidation conditions rather than trade signals.

### 9. Dataset validation
Checks OHLC integrity, timestamp ordering, duplicates, timezone state, range consistency and timeframe alignment.

## Required distinctions

- observation ≠ interpretation
- interpretation ≠ hypothesis
- historical frequency ≠ prediction
- scenario ≠ recommendation
- confidence reflects evidence coverage, not certainty
- chronology ≠ causality

## Removed by design

- live execution
- paper trading
- broker adapters
- position management
- execution risk gates
