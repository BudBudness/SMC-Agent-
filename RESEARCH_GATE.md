## Gate 6 — Research limitations
The benchmark uses rule-generated reference labels, not human ground truth. Human annotation and independent-source cross-validation remain research-quality upgrades.

Historical analogue results are descriptive only. Similarity does not imply causality or future direction.

No profitability, predictive-accuracy, recommendation, or live-readiness claim is made.
